# Customer Communications Library — Five Star Bath Solutions of Ottawa

Every message below follows the same shape: **what happened → what you need to know → whether you need to act → one link → one phone number.** Plain language, short sentences, one primary action. `[Portal Link]` always points to the relevant section of the customer's own portal, not the portal homepage, so the customer never has to hunt. `[Office Phone]` is a tap-to-call number on every message.

Placeholders: `{FirstName}`, `{Date}`, `{Time}`, `{RepName}`, `{Address}`, `{Amount}`, `{Balance}`, `{PortalLink}`, `{OfficePhone}` = a real Ottawa local number, `{ProjectType}`.

## Writing rules applied throughout (for anyone adding new messages later)

1. Company name appears in the first sentence of every SMS and every email subject/opening line.
2. One link only, always pointing to the specific relevant portal section — never a generic homepage, never multiple competing links.
3. One phone number, always the same office number, always described as a real way to reach a person.
4. A tentative date is never described using confirming language ("your installation on {Date}") — it always includes a qualifier ("tentative," "holding," "not yet confirmed").
5. No message asks the customer to create an account or remember a password.
6. No message implies a warranty determination, discount, or liability decision has been made — those always come from a human, separately.
7. Reading level target: plain, short sentences, no industry jargon (no "COI," "PO," "COD," "change order" without a plain-language gloss if it must appear in a customer message at all).
8. Any message that asks the customer to pay or click a payment link always names the exact project, address, and amount, and always routes through the portal rather than a bare external link — this is a deliberate anti-phishing safeguard for an older customer base who are an explicit target of e-Transfer-style scams.
9. No message assumes English is the customer's preferred language without checking their recorded language preference (see `06-future-state-operating-model.md` §13 and `07-system-integration-design.md` data model) — Ottawa's bilingual context means this field exists from Phase 1 even before a full French translation set ships.
