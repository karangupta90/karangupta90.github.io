# Success Metrics — Five Star Bath Solutions of Ottawa

A deliberately small initial set — one dashboard, not an excessive metrics sprawl. Each metric maps to a phase and has an obvious owner.

## Initial metric set (Phase 1–5 launch)

| Metric | Why it matters | Target (initial, adjust after baseline) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appointment acknowledgement rate | Core accountability fix | ≥ 95% acknowledged before deadline | Management |
| Average acknowledgement time | Speed-to-response for customers | Trend downward month over month | Management |
| Post-appointment result completion rate | Ensures proposals/designs/photos aren't lost | ≥ 95% same-day | Management |
| Missing 3D-design rate | Named as a specific failure point in the brief | < 2% of completed appointments | Management |
| Signed-contract delivery success rate (email + portal) | Replaces the expiring-link failure mode | ≥ 99% successful delivery, 100% eventual resend on failure | Office |
| Contract resend requests | Should trend toward zero once portal access is trusted | Downward trend | Office |
| Customer portal usage (active magic-link opens per project) | Confirms customers actually use the single destination instead of calling | Increasing over first 90 days | Management |
| Customer status calls to office | Should fall as the portal absorbs "where's my stuff" questions | Downward trend | Office |

## Phase 6–9 additions (once installation/service/commission phases are live)

| Metric | Why it matters | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Installation reschedule rate | Signals whether tentative/confirmed labeling is working | Production |
| Service-call acknowledgement time | Named explicitly, especially for safety/leak cases | Management |
| Service-call resolution time | Core service quality measure | Management |
| Repeat/reopened service-call rate | Feeds installation-quality and training decisions | Management |
| Integration failure rate | Reliability of the sync layer | Management/Ops |
| Manual-entry reduction (self-reported staff time on admin follow-up) | Confirms the "low administrative burden" objective is real, not theoretical | Management |

## Deferred to later (do not build a dashboard for these yet)

Employee adoption depth, review-conversion rate, and detailed AI-performance metrics are tracked qualitatively until Phases 9–10 are live — adding them earlier would exceed the "manageable initial set" instruction.
