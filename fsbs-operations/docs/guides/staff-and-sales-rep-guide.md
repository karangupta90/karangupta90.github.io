# Staff Guide &amp; Sales-Representative Guide

## For sales representatives

**Every appointment, two required actions:**
1. **Acknowledge it** — tap one of the five responses (Received & reviewed / Need more info / Scheduling conflict / Info looks wrong / Unable to attend) as soon as you see it, and always before 11:00 PM the day it's assigned (or within 2 hours if assigned that same evening). Just opening the appointment does not count.
2. **Submit your post-appointment result** by 9:00 PM the day of the appointment — pick the outcome first; the form only asks what's relevant to that outcome.

**Every completed appointment needs:**
- Photos in CompanyCam (confirmed in the result form).
- A 3D design uploaded (image, PDF, or a link to the design tool) — required even for a "pitch and miss," not just a sale.
- If sold: contract signed and deposit collected are both confirmed in the result form; a missing item doesn't block your submission, but it will show up on the management dashboard, so don't rely on "no one will notice."

**If you're behind:** save a draft rather than leaving the form untouched — a draft still shows as overdue, but it shows you're working on it, and management can see the difference.

**If you disagree with a returned/corrected submission:** the correction note explains exactly what to fix — resubmit rather than arguing it away; your original submission is preserved in history either way.

## For office staff

- You still book appointments manually — nothing about this system changes that.
- Once booked, the system handles confirmation, reminders, and rep-acknowledgement chasing — you don't need to personally follow up unless something escalates to Tier 2.
- If a signed-contract email fails to deliver, you'll get an alert with a one-click resend — always resend from there, never manually re-export/re-attach a different copy, so the version history stays accurate.
- Record consent (SMS/email marketing at booking, photo/video at the start of the in-home visit) — this is a real compliance requirement (CASL/PIPEDA), not paperwork for its own sake.

## For production staff

- A sold job only counts as a complete handoff when the checklist (contract ✓, design ✓, photos ✓, deposit ✓) is clear — an incomplete handoff shows on the dashboard, so chase the missing item rather than starting work on a guess.
- Only mark an installation date "Confirmed" once it is genuinely locked in — "Tentative"/"Reserved" language exists specifically so you're never the reason a customer was told a false certainty.
