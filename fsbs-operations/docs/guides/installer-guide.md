# Installer Guide

## Submitting an invoice/payment request

Kept deliberately short — 5 fields or fewer, so you're never tempted to just call the office instead:

1. Project/customer (pick from your assigned jobs).
2. Amount requested.
3. Category: standard project pay / approved additional work / materials purchased / travel.
4. Photo of the receipt (if claiming materials/travel).
5. Optional one-line note.

## What happens after you submit
`Submitted → Under review → (Correction requested, if something's unclear) → Approved (full or partial) or Rejected → Scheduled for payment → Paid.` You can see your own status at any time; you cannot see other installers' pay or company margins.

## Service callbacks
If you're asked to return for a warranty/callback visit, that work is tracked as a separate service call, not folded into the original job record — this keeps repeat-callback statistics accurate and is used to improve training and vendor decisions, not to single anyone out unfairly.
